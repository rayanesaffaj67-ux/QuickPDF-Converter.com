  3. Reorder the images with the arrows, or remove any you do not need.

Page size and orientation explained

A4 is the standard paper size in most countries and works well for printing and emailing. US Letter is the standard in the United States and Canada. Fit to image makes every PDF page match the size of its image, which is ideal for screenshots and photos you do not want to crop or add margins to. Choose portrait for upright documents and landscape for wide images.

Tips for a better PDF

  • Photograph documents in good light, holding the camera straight above the page.
  • Name your files 1, 2, 3 before adding them if you want a predictable page order.
  • Use portrait for documents and landscape for wide images such as screenshots.
  • Check the size limit of the website where you will upload the PDF. Smaller photos make smaller PDFs.
