Why convert JPG to PDF?

JPG is the format most phone cameras and scanners produce, so photos of receipts, homework, contracts and ID cards are usually JPG files. Combining them into one PDF makes them easy to print, email or upload to an application form.

A PDF looks the same on every device, keeps pages in a fixed order and is accepted by almost every office, school and government website. Sending one PDF is also easier than attaching a dozen separate pictures.

Tips for a better PDF

  • Take photos of documents in good light and hold the camera straight above the page.
  • Name your files 1, 2, 3 before adding them if you want a predictable page order.
  • Use portrait for documents and landscape for wide images such as screenshots.
  • Check the size limit of the website where you will upload the PDF.
